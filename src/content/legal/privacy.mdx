## The short version

- **No ads, no analytics, no trackers.** We don't sell or share data for marketing, and we don't use third-party
  cookies.
- **Browsing, downloading and using the API need no account.** We only keep what we need to run the site, count
  downloads fairly and stop abuse, and IP addresses are never stored as they are.
- **With an account**, we keep your username, your email address, a hash of your password (or the id of the
  Discord, GitHub or Google account you sign in with), your sessions, and what you publish.
- **You can export your data and delete your account** at any time from your [account page](/account).

## When you browse

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Technical logs: page, status, response time, a request id | Run and debug the site | <Placeholder>log retention, e.g. 14 days</Placeholder> |
| Your IP address, in memory only | Rate limits against abuse (logins, comments, downloads) | Minutes, never written to disk |
| A keyed hash of your IP address, the handler and the day | Count a download once per person and per day | 48 hours |

The logs never contain IP addresses, cookies, passwords or email addresses. A keyed hash (HMAC) can't be turned back
into an address, and the key never leaves the server.

### Images and videos from other sites

Game covers and screenshots load from IGDB (`images.igdb.com`) and Steam (`steamstatic.com`): your browser asks
them for the images, so they see your IP address, like for any image on the web. Videos in the docs only load from
YouTube (`youtube-nocookie.com`) when you click on them.

## When you have an account

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Username, email address, sign-up date, whether your email is verified | Your account, sign-in, password resets, reply notifications | Until you delete the account |
| A hash of your password (bcrypt) | Sign-in. We never see or keep the password itself | Until you change it or delete the account |
| For Discord, GitHub or Google sign-in: the provider's account id, and your username there (Discord, GitHub) | Sign-in, linking accounts | Until you unlink it or delete the account |
| Sessions: a hash of the session token, dates, your browser's user agent (shortened) | Keep you signed in, show you your sessions | 30 days after your last visit, or until you sign out |
| Verification and reset links (hashed) | Confirm your email, reset your password | 1 to 48 hours, deleted a week later |
| Your role, and a ban reason if a moderator banned you | Moderation | Until you delete the account |
| Moderation log: actions on accounts and content, with a keyed hash of the IP address | Security and accountability | 2 years |

Sign-in providers only tell us what's listed above. We never receive your password there, and we don't keep their
access tokens.

## What you publish

Handlers, versions, descriptions, comments and stars are what the hub is made of. Your username, your handlers,
your comments and your sign-up date are **public**. Reports you send to the moderators are not public; when you
report without being signed in, we keep a keyed hash of your IP address to stop duplicate reports, and clear it 30
days after the report is closed.

## Emails

We send emails only for your account: address verification, password resets, a change of email address, and
replies to your comments (you can turn those off in your account). <Placeholder>email provider</Placeholder> sends

## Cookies

Only the ones the site needs to work, so there is no cookie banner:

| Cookie | What for | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| `__Host-ssm_session` | Keeps you signed in | 30 days, renewed while you use the site |
| `__Host-ssm_oauth` | Protects a Discord, GitHub or Google sign-in while it happens | 10 minutes |

No cookie is set while you're not signed in or signing in.

## Deleting your account

From your [account page](/account). We then delete your sessions, linked sign-in accounts, pending links and
stars, remove your email address and password, replace your username with `deleted-` and a few characters, and
erase the text of your comments. Handlers you published stay on the hub, shown as made by "Deleted user", because
players rely on them; ask us if you want them removed too. Moderation log entries are kept for their 2 years.
