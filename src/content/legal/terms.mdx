## The service

SplitScreen.Me is a free community site: the documentation of Nucleus Co-op, the hub of handlers made by players,
accounts to publish and discuss them, and a public API. It's run by volunteers, without ads or payments. Using it
means accepting these terms; if you don't, please don't use it.

## Your account

- One account per person. Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for what's done with your account.
- Use an email address you can read: it's how you get password resets and important messages.
- Your username must not impersonate someone else or be offensive.
- You can delete your account at any time from your [account page](/account).

## Content rules

These apply to handlers, packages, descriptions, release notes, comments and usernames.

**Handlers and packages**

- A handler must do what its page says, for the game it's listed under, and nothing else.
- **No malware**: nothing that harms a player's PC, steals data, mines, spies, or downloads and runs code that isn't
  in the package. Explain in the description anything unusual a handler does (running as administrator, creating
  Windows users, changing system settings).
- **No pirated content**: no files of the game itself, and nothing whose purpose is to let people play a game they
  don't own. The network emulators Nucleus Co-op uses to connect copies of a game you own are fine.
- Only include files you have the right to share, and credit the work you build on.
- Don't publish someone else's handler as yours. Improving an existing handler is welcome: say so, and credit its
  author.

**Comments and everything else**

- Be kind: no harassment, hate, threats or personal attacks.
- No spam, advertising or links to malware or pirated games.
- No one else's personal information.
- Nothing illegal, and nothing sexual or violent beyond what a game page reasonably needs.

## Moderation

- New handlers are checked by a moderator before they're listed; trusted authors skip that step. Moderators also
  **verify** versions. Verification means a moderator looked at that version; it is not a guarantee.
- Moderators can hide a handler, remove a version or a comment, and suspend or ban accounts that break these rules.
  Every such action is logged.
- Anyone can **report** a handler or a comment. Reports go to the moderators, who decide what to do.
