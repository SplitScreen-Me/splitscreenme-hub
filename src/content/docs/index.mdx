## New to SplitScreen.Me?

Nucleus Co-op runs one copy of a PC game for each player, gives every controller, keyboard or mouse to its own
copy, and tiles the windows on your screen. It works with games that already have LAN or online multiplayer, and
the community writes a [handler](/doc/handlers) for each game so you don't have to configure anything. It's free,
open source, and has no account, no ads and no catch. Read [what SplitScreen.Me is](/doc/about), or go straight to
the [hub](/hub) and find your game.
