In the lobby, every monitor connected to your PC appears as a screen, with its resolution. Each screen has its own
layout, and players can be spread over several screens.

## Layouts

Pick a layout with the buttons under a screen:

| Layout | Players on that screen |
|---|---|
| Full screen | 1 |
| Top and bottom | 2, one above the other |
| Side by side | 2, left and right |
| 2 × 2 | 4 |
| **More ›** Six players | 6, in 2 rows and 3 columns |
| **More ›** Eight players | 8, in 2 rows and 4 columns |
| **More ›** Sixteen players | 16, in 4 rows and 4 columns |
| **More ›** Custom grid | The rows and columns set in **Settings › Layout** |
| **More ›** Manual | Players dropped anywhere on the screen |

Side by side gives each player a tall window, top and bottom a wide one. Wide windows suit most games better,
since games are made for wide screens.

## Three players (or five, or seven)

With three players on a 2 × 2 layout, one place stays empty. Give that space to a player with the grow button next
to their name in the side panel ("Grow over the free neighbouring places"): their window stretches over the free
place, so one player gets half of the screen and the others a quarter each. The same button shrinks it back.

For finer control, select a player's place and press <Kbd>R</Kbd> (**X** on a controller) to resize it: the arrows
move a corner, <Kbd>X</Kbd> switches to the other corner, and <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> (**A**) is done.

## Custom grid

**Settings › Layout** sets the custom grid: the number of horizontal and vertical divisions. For example two
vertical divisions and one horizontal give a grid of 3 columns and 2 rows, so 6 places. Then choose
**More › Custom grid** under a screen. Profiles keep their own copy of these settings.

## Manual

**More › Manual** lets you drop each player anywhere on the screen and resize their window freely. Use it for
unusual screens or for a big main view with smaller side windows.

## Several monitors

Drag players onto the screen you want them on. Some setups that work well:

- **One player per monitor**: full screen on each.
- **A TV and a monitor**: two players top and bottom on the TV, one on the monitor.
- **Two monitors, four players**: side by side on each monitor.

Keep the same display scaling on all monitors, ideally 100 %.

## Behind the windows

Some games can't fill their place exactly (fixed aspect ratios, minimum sizes). In **Settings › Layout**:

- **Split-screen division** fills the screen behind the game windows with a colour (black by default), so the
  desktop never shows through. **Hide desktop only** shows that background without resizing or moving the windows.
- **Cutscenes mode** (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>M</Kbd>) is for the moments a game plays a cutscene in every window:
  it can mute the other windows, keep their size, or unfocus them.
- **Windows merger** shows every copy in one window, which lets an upscaler such as Lossless Scaling work on the
  whole split screen. It doesn't support several monitors yet.

<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>R</Kbd> puts the windows back in place if a game moves or resizes its window during the
session, and <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>N</Kbd> switches the layout while you play.

Windows look stretched or blurry? See [Performance](/doc/troubleshooting/performance#stretched-or-blurry-windows).
