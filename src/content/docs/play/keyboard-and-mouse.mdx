Keyboards and mice come in two flavours with Nucleus Co-op, and each handler says which one it supports. On the
[hub](/hub), the game page lists it, and the catalogue in the app can filter on **Keyboard and mouse** and
**Several keyboards**.

| Handler supports | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Keyboard and mouse** | One player can use the PC's keyboard and mouse; everyone else uses a controller. That player plays in the window that has the focus |
| **Several keyboards and mice** | Every player can have their own keyboard and mouse, and they can be mixed with controllers |

## Joining with a keyboard and a mouse

Keyboards and mice appear in the lobby once you use them: press a key, or move a mouse. A keyboard joins the next
free place with a key press. To give that player a mouse, drag the mouse onto them; the player card then shows
both devices.

Seeing more keyboards than you plugged in? Gaming keyboards, mice with programmable buttons and laptop touchpads
often register as several devices. Use the one that lights up when you type, and leave the others in the tray.

## Locking inputs with End

With several keyboards and mice, Windows still has a single cursor and a single focused window. Nucleus Co-op
works around it once you **lock the inputs**:

1. Launch, and wait until every window is in place. Don't touch the mice while windows are being placed.
2. Press <Kbd>End</Kbd>. Each keyboard and mouse now talks to its own player's window only. The Windows cursor is
   parked, and each window shows its own cursor when its player moves their mouse or clicks.
3. Play.
4. Press <Kbd>End</Kbd> again to unlock and get your normal cursor back, for example to use another program.

The session screen shows whether inputs are locked, and has a **Lock inputs** button. Some handlers lock the
inputs by themselves once every window is ready, and some hide the taskbar while inputs are locked.

<Callout type="tip" title="Stuck with a locked cursor?">

Press <Kbd>End</Kbd> to unlock. If that doesn't help, <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Q</Kbd> closes Nucleus Co-op and every
copy of the game, and <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>+<Kbd>Alt</Kbd>+<Kbd>Del</Kbd> always gives you Windows back. If a game uses the
End key itself, change the lock key in **Settings › Keyboard shortcuts**.

</Callout>

## Good to know

- **Click once in your window** after locking if a camera keeps turning or a window ignores its mouse.
- **The first player is often the keyboard player** in handlers that support one keyboard and mouse: check the
  handler notes.
- **Reminder at launch.** Nucleus explains the lock key when you launch a session with keyboards and mice. Turn
  it off with **Settings › General › Don't remind me to lock inputs**.
- **Not every game can do it.** Many games read the keyboard and mouse in ways that can't be split per window,
  and some only accept one mouse for the camera. If the hub doesn't list several keyboards and mice for a game,
  don't expect it to work.
